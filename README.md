# FSEA -Emotion Analysis Enhancement with Fuzzy Signature 
In this study, we introduced a hybrid fuzzy signature-based approach to ana-lyze and model emotions derived from Plutchik wheel of emotions. The Fuzzy Signature Emotion Analysis (FSEA) model was illustrated on love as a complex emotion derived from six key sub-emotions: serenity, joy, ecstasy, acceptance, trust, and admiration. Then, we aggregated different tree structures (observa-tions) to accommodate for potential discrepancies in human judgments (annota-tions). Using fuzzy signature sets, we captured the overlapping nature of the sub emotions embedded in textual data, allowing smooth transitions (and better rep-resentation) between different levels of emotional intensity.
We applied and extended the FSEA model on all emotions spectrum (Plutchik wheel) using Mamdani inference system integrated with Emotion Distribution Learning. The FSEA model was utilized to generate a labeled dataset, tested and compared with Twitter Emotion Corpus on LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) and Bidirectional GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit) models for emotion detection. Moreover, the FSEA showed good results with transformer-based models. 
Indeed, the FSEA exhibits the ability to capture the ingrained ambiguity in tex-tual emotional states and improved emotion labeling. We are eager to enhance the FSEA’s results with transformer-based model and extend this study in sentiment analysis and fraud detection where accurately detecting emotional state is vital.

## Problem Formulation
Humans express their emotions by words. Emotion analysis is a method to identify emotional status. Emotion Distribution Learning (EDL) translates human emotions by considering multiple intensities conveyed in data format. Generally, emotion detection is more complex than just labeling it under six categories: love, fear, joy, sadness, surprise and anger. Indeed, capturing the inherent uncertainty and vagueness of human emotions is a complex task as presented in Plutchik wheel of emotions. In this paper we propose a novel emotion classification (analysis) framework; the Fuzzy Signature Emotion Classification (FSEC) model. FSEC facilitates Fuzzy Signature (FSig) to dy-namically conduct structural trees depending on the Plutchik wheel of emo-tions, integrated with the classical EDL approach to enhance emotion classi-fication classical methods  (to some extent). We applied for FSEC on a Re-current Neural Networks (RNN) emotion labeling model and compared it with the state-of-the-art. The results show more efficient detection of the overlapping embedded emotion cues in the dataset (tweets dataset) with high accuracy and F1 score (source code)
